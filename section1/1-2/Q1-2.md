# Section 1 Exercise 1-2 | Your First Agent Definition

## Goal

Define your first **Agent** — the smallest building block in CrewAI — with your own hands.
Experience how `role` / `goal` / `backstory` together become the agent's "personality."

## What you'll learn in this exercise

### An Agent is "a worker given a role"

In CrewAI, the first thing you do is decide **who is going to do the work**. That's the Agent.
An Agent's personality is defined by three pieces of text, which become the prompt sent to the LLM.

| Attribute | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `role` | Title / role | `"Technology Researcher"` |
| `goal` | What they want to achieve | `"Produce accurate research that beginners can understand"` |
| `backstory` | Persona / background | `"You are a researcher with 10 years of experience who…"` |

```python
from crewai import Agent, LLM

llm = LLM(model="gemini/gemini-flash-latest")

researcher = Agent(
    role="Technology Researcher",
    goal="Produce accurate research that beginners can understand",
    backstory="You are a research expert who is great at breaking down difficult technology into simple terms.",
    llm=llm,
    verbose=True,
)
```

> **Key point**: `backstory` has the strongest influence on tone, perspective, and expertise. Change it and the whole feel of the output shifts.

### An Agent doesn't do anything on its own

An Agent is a "worker," so it needs **something to do (a Task)** and **a place to run (a Crew)**.
This starter already has the Task and Crew set up for you, so you can focus purely on **defining the Agent** (you'll learn Task/Crew in 1-3 and 1-4).
